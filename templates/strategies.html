How to read this

Raw is expectancy before trading costs, which answers one question: does the strategy predict direction at all? Kept is what would actually have ended up in your account once fees and spread came out.

A wide gap between the two columns means the strategy works but the instrument or interval is too expensive to trade that way. That gap is the most common reason retail day trading loses money. It is also why the default ranking is raw skill: sorting by what you keep across many instruments mostly sorts by which symbols are cheap, not by which strategies are good.
