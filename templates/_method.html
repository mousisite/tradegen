How we tested
  • Up to 5 years of daily prices for each stock and coin.
  • Every time the strategy leaned up or down, we took a pretend trade that way. Stop {{ method.stop }} ATR away, target {{ method.target }} ATR away, closed after {{ method.days }} trading days if it hit neither. ATR is how much the price usually moves in a day.
  • Worked means it reached the target before the stop. If both were touched on the same day, we count it as a loss.
  • Costs come off every trade: {{ method.equity }}% a round trip for stocks, {{ method.crypto }}% for crypto.
  • Kept a trade is in R, the amount risked. +0.10R means 10 cents kept for every dollar risked, on average, after costs.
  • Strategies that fired fewer than {{ method.min }} times on a stock are left out: that few is luck.

Re-run every day. Past results do not promise future ones. This is software output, not financial advice.
