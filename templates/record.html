How it is scored
  • Each stock's call is written down once a day, with the price at the time.
  • Then we pretend to buy at that price, with the same stop and target the app uses everywhere: {{ method.stop }} ATR below and {{ method.target }} ATR above. ATR is how much the price usually moves in a day.
  • If it said BUY, it was right when the price reached the target before the stop, or ended at least half a day's move higher after {{ method.days }} trading days. It was wrong when the stop was hit first, or the price ended lower.
  • If it said WAIT or AVOID, the same pretend buy is scored the other way round: right when that buy would have lost, wrong when it would have made money.
  • A price that ended about where it started counts as neither.
  • Calls are never edited or removed.

Past results do not promise future ones. This is software output, not financial advice.
