What your broker charges

the setting that changes the verdict most
Basis points, in and out combined. 2 is typical for a commission-free broker paying only the spread.
20 means 0.1% taker fee on each side, which is normal on a retail exchange.

Why this dominates everything. On five-minute crypto bars, a 20 basis point round trip costs about 1.4 times the entire stop distance. That pushes the break-even win rate near 98%, which nothing achieves. Put your real numbers here before you trust a single call.
