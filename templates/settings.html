What your broker charges

the setting that changes the verdict most
Basis points, in and out combined. 2 is typical for a commission-free broker paying only the spread.
20 means 0.1% taker fee on each side, which is normal on a retail exchange.
For a contract such as ES=F. One tick of slippage and a commission of about five dollars come to about 0.6 on ES and 0.8 on the micro contracts.

Why this dominates everything. On five-minute crypto bars, a 20 basis point round trip costs about 1.4 times the entire stop distance. That pushes the break-even win rate near 98%, which nothing achieves. Put your real numbers here before you trust a single call.

How trades are built

Daily is the only interval measured with positive expectancy so far.
In ATR, the instrument's own volatility unit. Wider stops survive noise but risk more per unit.
Also in ATR. Together with the stop this sets your reward to risk.
Bars a setup gets to work before it is closed at whatever it is worth. auto scales it to the interval, which matters: a fixed 24 bars is two hours on 5-minute bars but five weeks on daily ones.
Some people find red candles push them to act. The calm colours show the same prices.

Your trading rules

checked before the journal logs a trade
In R, your risk per trade: 2 means two full losses. After costs, counted on trades closed that New York day. 0 switches it off.
Counted by when you entered, New York date. 0 switches it off.
A New York time, like 10:30. Leave it empty to switch it off.

When a rule says stop, the journal asks before it logs another trade. You can still log it, and it is marked as one that broke a rule, so the breakdown shows what breaking it cost.
