This describes what Orenth actually does with data. It is written to be checkable against the source rather than to be comprehensive, and where a thing does not happen it says so plainly instead of staying silent.

What is collected

If you sign in with Google, Orenth receives and stores:

  • Your email address, used as the identity of your account.
  • Your name and profile picture, used to show who is signed in.
  • A Google account identifier, so that the account still resolves if your email address changes.

That is the whole of it. Orenth asks Google only for the openid, email and profile scopes. It cannot read your Gmail, Drive, contacts or calendar, and it never sees your Google password.

What you create

Anything you enter is stored against your account: trades, watchlist, alerts, saved theses, and the history of analyses you have run. Only you can see it. Other people signed into the same installation cannot read it, and that separation is enforced by the database query rather than by the interface hiding things.

One thing is deliberately shared: the measured strategy record, which says how a given strategy has behaved on a given instrument. It contains no personal information, describes markets rather than people, and is what makes the analysis better as more of it accumulates.

Where data goes

To produce an analysis, Orenth sends the instrument you asked about to the sources it reads. It does not send your identity, your positions or your journal to any of them.

What does not happen

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking scripts. The pages load no third-party JavaScript at all.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of personal data with anyone.
  • No cookies except the one that keeps you signed in. It holds an opaque session token and nothing else.

Keeping and deleting

Your data stays until you remove it. Every dataset can be downloaded as CSV or JSON from the Account page at any time, without asking anyone. Deleting your account removes your trades, watchlist, alerts, theses and analysis history immediately and permanently; the shared strategy record stays, because it was never yours alone and contains nothing about you.

Sessions expire after {{ session_days }} days. Signing out ends the session on the server rather than only in the browser.

Security

Sign-in uses Google's authorization code flow with PKCE, exchanged between servers. Session tokens are random, opaque and stored, so they can be revoked. Credentials are read from the environment and are never written to a configuration file.

What this is

Orenth is analysis software. It reads public market data, filings, news and social posts, applies a set of strategies to them, back-tests those strategies against the instrument's own history, and reports what it found.

What it is not

It is not financial advice, and nothing it produces is a recommendation to buy or sell anything. It does not know your circumstances, your tax position, your other holdings or your obligations, and it is not regulated to advise you about any of them. It has no connection to any broker and never places an order.

What the numbers mean

Every probability, expectancy and confidence interval is measured from historical price data. Past behaviour is not a promise about the future, and a measured edge can stop existing without warning. The software says so in its own output: when a setup has no demonstrated edge it reports that rather than inventing confidence, and by its own measurements most short-horizon trading loses money once costs are charged.

Day trading loses money for the large majority of retail participants. This tool's own back-tests agree with that finding. Never risk money you cannot afford to lose.

Data accuracy

Market data comes from third parties and may be delayed, incomplete or wrong. Figures taken from filings are shown with a link to the filing so you can check them. No warranty is given that any figure is correct, and decisions made on them are yours.

Availability

The service may be unavailable, and scheduled checks do not run while it is. Alerts are a convenience, not a guarantee: never rely on one to exit a position.

Your account

You are responsible for what happens under your account. You may export your data or delete your account at any time from the Account page. Access may be withdrawn for abuse of the service or of the data sources it depends on.

Liability
